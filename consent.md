# Introduction

You are invited to participate in an academic interview examining the privacy and security risks involved in the sharing of LLM application accounts. Given your experience in this domain, we cordially invite you to participate in this interview. Please read the following information carefully before deciding whether to participate. If you have any questions, please feel to ask the researcher. 

## Purpose of the interview

This interview aims to explore the current ecosystem of AI accounts' sharing practices, especially sharing LLM application accounts. We focus on analyzing the privacy and security challenges encountered during these processes and understanding how users perceive and address these risks. Your valuable insights will help the academic community better understand and govern privacy and security issues in AI application scenarios. 

## Study procedure

If you agree to participate, you will take part in a one-to-one interview with a researcher. We will ask about your experiences with sharing LLM application accounts, your use cases, your awareness of potential privacy and security risks, and the mitigation strategies. The interview is expected to last approximately 30 to 60 minutes. We will audio-record the interview, if you grant explicit consent for us to do so.

## Potential risks and discomforts

This interview only involves your general knowledge and experiences regarding sharing LLM application accounts, as well as your thoughts on privacy and security. It does not solicit highly sensitive personally identifiable information, financial passwords, or trade secrets. You may refuse to answer any question at any time during the interview if you feel uncomfortable or believe it touches upon sensitive information, without needing to provide any justification.

## Privacy and confidentiality

We are committed to maintaining strict confidentiality of the information you provide. All audio recordings and transcripts generated from the interview will be anonymized and de-identified. The original data will be encrypted and securely stored on protected devices, where access is strictly restricted to members of the research team for analysis purposes only. The findings of this research will only present aggregated analyses and anonymized quotes. No information that could identify you personally will be disclosed.

## Voluntary participation

You participation in this interview is entirely voluntary. You have the right to withdraw from the interview at any time. You may also request that your interview data be withdrawn and destroyed after the interview is completed. Choosing not to participate or withdrawing will not result in any negative consequences.
