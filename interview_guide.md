We designed the interview to be neutral and non-leading, with the following questions:

1. What types of LLM application accounts have you shared?

2. What motivated you to share LLM application accounts?

3. How you determine that with whom you share LLM application accounts?

4. What did you do with these shared LLM application accounts?

5. Do you perceive any privacy or security risks associated with these shared account? Could you elaborate on why or why not?

6. What type of information did you input into the shared account, and into a non-shared account?

7. Are there other risks or issues associated with these shared account?

8. Through which channels, if any, did you learn about these potential privacy and security risks?

9. What, if any, personal measures or actions to address or mitigate these potential risks?

10. How do you manage your chat history or memories when using a shared account?

11. Have you ever observed inputs, prompts or histories of other users? If so, how did you respond and does this impact your own usage behavior?

13. What challenges or issues, if any, do you think currently exist in this ecosystem, particularly concerning privacy and security?

14. What are your expectations, if any, regarding how these issues should be resolved in the future?
